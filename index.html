🌿 HairUp: Privacy Policy & Support

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Overview & Data Privacy Commitment

HairUp ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy and is fully committed to safeguarding your personal information. HairUp is designed from the ground up as a private hair wellness, habit tracker, and scalp care companion.

2. Data Collection and Local Storage

  • On-Device Storage: All your personal hair analysis responses, questionnaire data, biological scores, and Before/After comparison photographs are stored strictly on your local device. We do not maintain external database copies of your photographs or health questionnaire inputs.
  • No User Accounts Required: You are not required to create an account, sign in, or provide personal identity credentials (such as national ID or home address) to use HairUp.
  • Camera and Photo Library Access: The app requests access to your camera and photo library exclusively when you choose to document your hairline density or track Before/After progress. Photos are never uploaded to any public server or sold to third parties.

3. App Tracking Transparency (ATT) & Analytics

  • Attribution & Analytics: We use Google Firebase Analytics to aggregate anonymous operational metrics (e.g., app crashes, feature usage frequencies) to enhance overall app stability.
  • Opt-In Consent: In accordance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, we request your permission before any tracking identifier is used for marketing campaign attribution. You can change this permission at any time in your iOS Device Settings.

4. In-App Purchases & Apple Subscriptions

All in-app purchases and subscriptions are handled securely through Apple's StoreKit framework and RevenueCat. HairUp never receives or stores your credit card number, bank details, or billing address. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled at any time through your Apple ID Account Settings.

5. Terms of Use & Medical Disclaimer

  • Standard EULA: HairUp applies Apple's Standard End User License Agreement (EULA). You can review the agreement here: Apple Standard EULA.
  • Medical Disclaimer: HairUp is an informational wellness and habit-tracking application based on scientific and dermatological literature. It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or cure. Always consult a qualified dermatologist or physician before starting any medical treatments.
